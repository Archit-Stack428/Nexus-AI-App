About

NEXUS AI
Version 1.0.0

Nexus AI is a premium, all-in-one smart assistant powered by high-speed neural network models. It features advanced multi-turn chat, PDF summary & Q&A assistant, optical character recognition (OCR) tool, real-time English/Hindi translation, and voice interface controls.

Core Capabilities

Smart AI Chat

Context-aware multimodal conversation interface

PDF Assistant

Summarize pages, extract key points & query PDFs

Image Intelligence

Deep visual content analysis & OCR text extractor

Bilingual Translation

Quick English and Hindi translation interface

Voice Synthesis

Speech recognition inputs and text-to-speech reading

Privacy Policy

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not send your personal information to any external servers for storage.

Authentication

Your password is securely hashed before storage. Authentication tokens are used to keep your session secure.

Chat History

Chat conversations are stored locally on your device. You can delete any conversation at any time from the Chat History section.

Voice Data

Voice input is processed using your device's built-in speech recognition. Audio is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

Translation uses the MyMemory API. PDF and image processing happens entirely in your browser. No files are uploaded to external servers.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, please contact us through the app settings.
